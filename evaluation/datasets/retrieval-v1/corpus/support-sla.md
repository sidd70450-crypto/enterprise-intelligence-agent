# Customer support service levels

## Priority definitions

Priority 1 means a customer cannot dispatch any shipments. Priority 2 means a major feature is degraded but a workaround exists. Priority 3 covers questions and minor defects.

## Response targets

For Priority 1 issues the first response must come within 15 minutes, at any hour, and an update is sent every hour until resolution. Priority 2 issues get a first response within four business hours. Priority 3 issues get a response within two business days.

## Service credits

If monthly availability of the planning platform falls below 99.9 percent, enterprise customers receive a service credit of 10 percent of that month's subscription fee. Below 99.0 percent the credit rises to 25 percent.
