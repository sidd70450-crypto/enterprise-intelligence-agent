# Engineering onboarding guide

## First week

New engineers receive a laptop with full-disk encryption already enabled and are paired with an onboarding buddy for their first month. By the end of the first week they should have shipped one small change to production.

## Code review

Every change needs approval from one reviewer who did not write it. Changes to payments or security code need two reviewers, one of them from the owning team.

## On-call

Engineers join the on-call rotation after three months. A rotation lasts one week, and on-call engineers receive a fixed allowance of 300 euros per week plus time off in lieu for any night call longer than one hour.
