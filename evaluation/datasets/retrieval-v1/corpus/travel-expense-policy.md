# Travel and expense policy

## Flights

Employees book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. For longer flights, premium economy is allowed. Business class needs written approval from a vice president.

## Hotels and meals

Hotel nightly limits are 180 euros in most cities and 260 euros in London, Paris and Zurich. The daily meal allowance is 55 euros; alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the trip, with a receipt for every item above 25 euros. Claims submitted later than 60 days are paid only with approval from the finance director.
