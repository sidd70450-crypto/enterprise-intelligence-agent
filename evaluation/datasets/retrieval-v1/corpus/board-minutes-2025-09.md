# Board meeting minutes, 18 September 2025

## Attendance

Seven of eight directors attended. The chair noted a quorum.

## Decisions

The board approved the 30 million euro expansion of the Gdansk warehouse, with construction to begin in the second quarter of 2026. The board declined the proposal to acquire a parcel locker start-up, judging the valuation too high.

The board asked management to present a plan for reducing the carbon emissions of the delivery fleet by 40 percent by 2030, including the cost of moving urban routes to electric vans.

## Next meeting

The next meeting is on 11 December 2025 in Rotterdam.
