# Data retention policy POL-114

Owner: Office of the Chief Privacy Officer. Applies to every system that stores customer or employee data.

## Retention periods

Shipment tracking events are kept for 25 months so that customers can compare a full year against the year before. Customer access logs are kept for 400 days and then deleted automatically by the nightly purge job. Payment records are retained for ten years, as tax law requires. Recordings of support calls are deleted after 90 days unless a dispute is open.

## Deletion requests

When a customer asks for their personal data to be erased, the privacy team must confirm the request within 72 hours and complete erasure within 30 days. Backups are not edited; instead, erased records are suppressed if a backup is ever restored.

## Exceptions

Legal hold overrides every period in this policy. Only the General Counsel can place or lift a legal hold.
