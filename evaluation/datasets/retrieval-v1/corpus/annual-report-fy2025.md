# Kestrel Logistics annual report, fiscal year 2025

## Financial summary

Kestrel Logistics closed fiscal year 2025 with total revenue of 412 million euros, up 18 percent on the previous year. Subscription software now contributes 61 percent of revenue, while one-off consulting work fell to 9 percent. Operating margin improved to 14.2 percent because cloud hosting costs per shipment dropped after the move to a shared routing cluster.

The Northern Europe region remained the largest market at 47 percent of revenue. The Iberia region grew fastest, doubling its customer count after the Lisbon office opened in March.

## Workforce

Headcount reached 1,340 employees at year end. Voluntary attrition was 7.5 percent, the lowest in five years, which management attributes to the four-day work week pilot in the Rotterdam depot.

## Outlook

For fiscal year 2026 the board expects revenue growth between 12 and 15 percent. The largest planned investment is a 30 million euro expansion of the automated warehouse in Gdansk.
